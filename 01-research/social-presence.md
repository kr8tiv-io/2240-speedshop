# 2240 Speed Shop — Social Presence Analysis

**Research date:** August 2, 2026
**Researcher:** Claude (social-presence research agent, website-rebuild project)
**Subject:** 2240 Speed Shop, 2009 91 Avenue NW, Edmonton, AB T6P 1L1 (east Edmonton, effectively the Sherwood Park boundary) — 780-999-6450 — 2240speedshop@gmail.com — https://2240speedshop.com

---

## 1. Executive Summary

2240 Speed Shop's social presence is **small, personal, and dormant** — and that is the single most important strategic fact for the rebuild. The entire footprint is one Instagram account (**242 followers, 17 posts, no post since Dec 2024** on their own feed), an empty auto-created Threads mirror, the owner's unbranded personal TikTok, and a personal (not business) Facebook profile. There is **no business Facebook page, no YouTube channel, no X/Twitter, and no branded TikTok**. The shop is run by **Terry Harmider** (posts as "Terry M"), and the social accounts reveal several things the current GoDaddy one-pager website does not: the "customs and classics" positioning, the Sherwood Park identity, the annual pilgrimage to the **Radium Hot Springs Columbia Valley Classics Show & Shine**, a much broader service list (tinting, detailing, upholstery, collision, engine/transmission work per Alignable), and a B2B referral network with Edmonton-area classic-car businesses including **Yesterdays Auto Gallery** (a three-time repeat customer).

The rebuild opportunity: the new site must become the brand's content home because the social channels cannot carry it. Everything findable — reels of the shop floor set to Metallica/AC/DC/GN'R, a photographer stopping traffic for their 1960s Dodge D-Series truck — points to an authentic old-school shop with strong visual material and zero digital marketing discipline.

## 3. What Social Reveals That the Website Doesn't

1. **Positioning: "customs and classics."** The IG bio and reel captions brand the shop as "2240 speed shop customs and classics" — a cleaner, more evocative positioning than the website's generic "innovative automotive solutions" copy. Recommend adopting it.
2. **Sherwood Park identity.** Captions and TikTok discover pages say **Sherwood Park, Alberta**; the address (2009 91 Ave NW, T6P 1L1) sits on Edmonton's eastern boundary. SEO must target BOTH "Edmonton" and "Sherwood Park" (+ Strathcona County).
3. **Radium / Columbia Valley Classics Show & Shine** — annual September event (Sept 19, 2026 next), 1,000+ classic cars at Radium Hot Springs, BC. The shop road-trips to it and it's their most search-associated event. Blog/content gold (event recaps, "getting your classic show-ready" service angle).
4. **Ten service lines the website never mentions** (per Alignable): window tinting, detailing, oil changes, collision repair, brakes, electrical, engine rebuilding, interior upholstery, transmission service. Each is a website service page + local SEO keyword the current one-pager throws away.
5. **The owner and the human story.** Terry Harmider — invisible on the website, but the entire personality of the social presence. An about/meet-the-owner page with the shop's rock-and-roll character would convert.
6. **Signature asset: the 1960s Dodge D-Series shop truck** — photogenic enough that a passing photographer pulled over for it. Natural hero image / brand mascot for the new site.
7. **B2B referral ecosystem:** Yesterdays Auto Gallery (repeat customer), Bright World Restoration, Street Rod Warehouse, Inline Automotive and Performance — partner/testimonial candidates and local backlink targets.
8. **Two photographer relationships** (Nathan Lajeunesse / @natlajphotography; "JG Photography") — sources for professional imagery for the rebuild.

## 4. Strategic Implications for the Rebuild

- **The website must be the content engine.** Social is dormant; a 50-post blog + strong Google Business Profile will do the heavy lifting. Embed the existing IG grid (it still shows well-shot reels) rather than promising fresh social content.
- **Biggest social gaps, in priority order:** (1) Facebook business Page — the classic-car demographic + Marketplace; (2) Google Business Profile optimization (only a 3/5 YellowPages rating found — reviews strategy needed); (3) branded TikTok/Reels cross-posting to capture the existing "2240speed shop radium car show" discover demand; (4) YouTube long-form build series (open local lane).
- **Voice guidance for copy:** keep it blue-collar and understated ("customs and classics"), lean on classic-rock energy visually, avoid corporate-speak — the current website copy ("innovative automotive solutions") is off-brand versus the authentic social voice.
- **Quick wins:** add the IG link to the site (currently zero cross-linking); fix hashtag usage; claim garagecar.ca redirect; get the Dodge D100 professionally shot by the photographers already in orbit.
