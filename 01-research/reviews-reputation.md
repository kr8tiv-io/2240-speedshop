## 1. Executive summary (read this first)

- **Google Business Profile is the ONLY live review surface — and it sits at 2.7 stars from 7 reviews** (3× five-star, 4× one-star). All four 1-star ratings are **drive-by ratings with zero text**. All three 5-star reviews have text and name the owner, Terry, personally.
- **The YellowPages.ca listing has been marked "permanently closed" and its detail page deleted** — but Google's index still holds a fragment of a devastating review from it accusing "Terry" of theft (full recovered text in §3.2). That listing carried a 3/5 rating before removal.
- **The shop does not exist on Yelp, BBB, Facebook (as a business page), Bing Places, Kijiji, 411.ca, Foursquare, or any auto-shop directory checked.** Zero Reddit or beyond.ca/forum chatter under any name variant. The citation footprint is essentially GBP + one dead YP listing.
- **Two abandoned/conflicting web properties**: the live site 2240speedshop.com (GoDaddy one-pager with a "JOIN THE LAUNCH" email-capture hero) and a **dead second domain, garagecar.ca**, still indexed by Google as "Home - 2240 Speedshop" with a different email (info@garagecar.ca).
- **Owner identified:** Terry (surname appears as **Terry Harmider** on the public Facebook profile that posts the shop's reels; Instagram display name "Terry M"). Every review — good and bad — is about Terry personally. The brand *is* Terry.
- **Net reputation picture:** a passionate one-man classic/custom shop with genuinely admired workmanship, carrying (a) an unanswered 1-star rating cluster on Google, (b) a serious theft allegation fossilized in search snippets, and (c) near-zero positive review volume to dilute either. For the rebuild, review generation + owner responses + citation building are as important as the website itself.

4. **See-it-to-believe-it workmanship** — "Go meet with Terry, look at the work that he does firsthand" → strong argument for a heavy build-gallery / project-log website.

### 5.2 Complaint themes / risks
1. **The theft-and-dishonesty allegation** (YP fragment, §3.2) — singular but severe, personal, and still indexed. Mitigation: bury via volume (reviews, fresh content, new citations), never engage publicly.
2. **Anonymous 1-star cluster on Google** (4 ratings, no text) — reads as a coordinated grudge; currently defines the 2.7 average because volume is so low.
3. **Zero owner responses** anywhere — looks unmanaged.
4. **No complaints exist about pricing, timelines, or quality from ordinary customers** on any platform — the negative record is entirely the one dispute + silent ratings.
