# 2240 Speed Shop — Edmonton-Area Competitor Landscape

**Project:** 2240speedshop.com rebuild — SEO strategy input
**Researched:** August 2, 2026 (live web searches + site fetches)
**Coverage area:** Edmonton, Sherwood Park, St. Albert, Leduc, Nisku, Spruce Grove, Stony Plain, Acheson

---

## 1. Executive summary

The Edmonton metro performance market splits into six clusters: (1) two SERP-dominating giants — **JBs Power Centre** (retail/install, since 1966) and the **Park Muffler / Park Performance / Player 3** ecosystem in Sherwood Park (1,900+ Google reviews); (2) full-service performance + dyno shops — **SSS Motorsports**, **Speedtech Racing Development**, **Acceleration Auto & Performance**, **Promax Performance**; (3) dyno/ECU tuning specialists — **CCMR Performance**, **Horsepower Solutions**, **King Tuner**, **Player 3**; (4) a deep diesel-performance bench — **OCDiesel**, **Revolution Motors**, **MHPD**, **Adrenaline Diesel**, **Big Rig Power**; (5) hot-rod/restoration shops that overlap 2240's vintage niche most directly — **The Iron Garage**, **Sideshow Fabrication & Paint**, **Wadson's Hot Rods**, **Joey's Place**, **So-Cal Speed Shop (Spruce Grove)**; and (6) engine builders/machine shops — **The Block Shop/TBS**, **Weldangrind**, **Best Engine Rebuilder**, **Alberta Cylinder Head**, **Extreme Engines**.

**Key strategic finding:** Nobody in Edmonton cleanly owns the combined position 2240 occupies — *vintage restoration + custom builds + modern performance upgrades under one "speed shop" brand*. The keyword "speed shop Edmonton" is contested only by JBs (heritage story, but now a big-box retailer, not a builder) and 2240 itself. The restoration shops rank poorly for performance terms; the performance shops don't target vintage/restoration terms. 2240's current site is thin (placeholder images, minimal content, no portfolio), a stray second domain (garagecar.ca, titled "Home - 2240 Speedshop") is dead/NXDOMAIN and should be cleaned up or 301'd, and 2240 has almost no visible review footprint (a lone 3/5 YellowPages rating; no visible Google review mass) — reviews are the single biggest local-pack gap vs. every ranked competitor.

## 3. Competitor profiles

### Tier A — SERP-dominating giants (retail-led, install bays attached)

#### JBs Power Centre — jbspowercentre.com
- **Locations:** South Edmonton + West Edmonton (and NE Calgary); 3 wholesale DCs, 200,000 sq ft warehousing. Founded **1966 as a small Edmonton speed shop** by Jim Bell.
- **Services:** performance parts retail/e-com, diesel performance (Duramax/Cummins/Power Stroke), turbo & supercharger install, carburetors, truck accessories, car audio, remote starters; MECP-certified installers.
- **Reviews:** West Edmonton ~4.0/746; Gateway ~4.8/174; 97 St (closed loc.) 3.9/296.
- **SEO strength:** Very high. Shopify collection pages rank for "diesel performance," "turbochargers," "carburetors" + city modifiers; huge brand search volume; sponsors Friday Night Street Legal drags, Miles of Mayhem, Spec-D drift — strong link/mention profile.
- **Why they compete with 2240:** they own the phrase "speed shop Edmonton" narrative (heritage story) and capture the parts-buying end of every project 2240 would build. They do NOT do restorations or custom vintage builds — that's 2240's wedge.
- **Related entity:** **Dix Performance North** (dixperformancenorth.com) — JBs' wholesale sibling (B2B, 220+ lines, SEMA member). Not a consumer competitor but adds domain authority to the family.

#### Park Muffler Radiator Brakes & Tires + Park Performance + Player 3 (Sherwood Park cluster)
- **Park Muffler** — parkmuffler.com, 101 Seneca Rd, Sherwood Park. Since 1979. **4.7–4.8 stars, ~1,939 reviews** (largest review mass in the market), BBB A+ since 1985.
  - Services: custom exhaust (Magnaflow/Flowmaster/Black Widow), performance upgrades/chips/tuners, diesel mechanic services, general repair.
  - SEO: service-page machine; ranks for "custom exhaust," "performance upgrades," "diesel repair" + Edmonton/Sherwood Park modifiers.
- **Park Performance** — parkperformance.ca, 141 Seneca Rd (same street). Since 2013. Performance parts + accessories retail, exhaust, tires, lift kits; install while you wait.
- **Player 3 Performance Tuning** — player3.ca. **Division of the Park group**; Chris Zelinsky; domestic ECM reflash (HP Tuners), authorized Texas Speed & Performance dealer (LS/Hemi parts), remote tunes from C$250.
- **Why they compete:** the cluster covers exhaust + parts + domestic tuning — three of 2240's revenue lines — with overwhelming review dominance in the eastern metro.

### Tier B — Full-service performance & tuning shops (closest service-mix rivals)

#### SSS Motorsports — sssmotorsports.ca (also sssjdm.com)
- **Location:** 9831 63 Ave NW, Edmonton. 780-438-2638.
- **Services:** Dynocom AWD 15000 roller dyno, custom tuning + standalone ECU (Link, Haltech, ECUMaster, Cobb, HP Tuners, Hondata, AEM), engine builds, custom fabrication, forced induction, **full restoration + body work**, alignments (drift/track), parts store, JDM sourcing.

- **SEO strength:** High. Two domains, dedicated /dyno-tuning and /performance landing pages that rank, active IG/FB/YouTube/TikTok, even sells a proprietary product (Subaru cyl-4 cooling mod). Best site in the service tier.
- **Why they compete:** the model 2240 should emulate — full-service performance with dyno + fabrication + restoration. Different vehicle culture (JDM vs. 2240's vintage/domestic), but they absorb generic "performance shop / dyno tuning Edmonton" demand.

- **Why they compete:** the premier pure-tuning brand in the metro for domestic/race builds — exactly who a restomod owner gets referred to for final tuning.

## 5. What this means for the 2240 rebuild (SEO implications)

1. **Claim "speed shop Edmonton" outright.** JBs ranks with an *about-us* page and is a retailer; 2240 is an actual speed shop and already appears on page 1. A strong homepage + heritage/brand story page + schema (AutoRepair/AutoPartsStore, LocalBusiness) can take #1.
2. **The vintage-performance intersection is empty.** No competitor targets "restomod Edmonton," "classic car performance upgrades Edmonton," "LS swap classic car Edmonton," "carbureted tuning Edmonton" (CCMR only brushes it). These map directly to 2240's actual work and should anchor the 50-blog plan.
3. **Weak incumbents on money terms:** "dyno tuning Edmonton" (#1 = free Wix site), "ECU tuning Edmonton" (#2 = UK doorway page), "custom exhaust Edmonton" (#1 = a single 2024 blog post). Dedicated, well-built service pages can crack all three within months.
4. **Reviews are the existential gap.** Every ranked local rival has 25–1,939 Google reviews; 2240 has effectively none visible. A review-generation system must launch with the site — the local pack is won on review mass before content ever matters.
5. **Copy the MHPD play:** their blog alone ranks a semi-truck shop for generic "dyno tuning Edmonton." Consistent technical content (build logs, dyno results, how-tos) will out-rank the service tier's mostly static sites.
6. **Portfolio is the conversion weapon.** Iron Garage, Sideshow, and Joey's win on project galleries; 2240's current site has placeholder images. Every build documented = SEO asset + proof.
7. **Housekeeping:** kill or 301 the stray garagecar.ca duplicate-brand domain (currently dead/NXDOMAIN but still indexed as "Home - 2240 Speedshop"); ensure NAP consistency (2009 91 Ave NW, T6P 1L1, 780-999-6450) across YellowPages/Yelp/Google.
