# 2240 Speed Shop — Blog / Informational Keyword Research
**Project:** 2240speedshop.com rebuild — SEO content plan input (feeds 50-blog plan)
**Market:** Edmonton, Alberta, Canada | **Researched:** August 2026
**Method:** Live SERP checks (Google via web search) on representative queries per cluster. Difficulty is judged by what actually ranks today: forum threads, marketplace listings, and PDFs = weak SERP = opportunity; local shop blogs ranking = proven model; national publishers/dealer content = harder.

---

## How to read this document

- **Target query** — the primary query; most topics have 3–10 sibling long-tails that one article captures.
- **Intent** — Informational (I), Commercial-investigation (CI), Local (L), Transactional-adjacent (T). Blend shown where relevant.
- **Difficulty** — LOW / LOW-MED / MED / MED-HIGH / HIGH, based on observed SERPs, *for a local shop with a real site, GBP, and genuine E-E-A-T*. A shop in Edmonton with photos of its own dyno/inspection bay outranks generic national content on any query with local or Canadian modifiers.
- **Angle** — the one-line editorial hook that beats what currently ranks.

**Shop context that shapes this plan:** 2240 Speed Shop's current positioning is *restorations + custom builds + performance upgrades, with a vintage/classic lean* ("Revive Your Ride"). The keyword set therefore spans BOTH modern performance (tuning, forced induction, exhaust) AND classic/restomod content — the classic cluster is nearly uncontested locally and matches the shop's actual identity.

**Key competitor observation from SERPs:** Park Muffler (Sherwood Park) already ranks with a blog post for diesel-delete queries; Revolution Motors ranks for "why out-of-province inspections fail." This proves the playbook works in this exact market — local shop blogs DO take these SERPs. Other Edmonton performance competitors (Horsepower Solutions, Player 3, SSS Motorsports, Acceleration Auto, Eurotekk) have little or no blog content = open field.

## Executive summary — the 10 biggest opportunities

1. **"Are exhaust modifications legal in Alberta?"** — current SERP is 2005-era Beyond.ca forum archives, a motorcycle forum, and a city councillor's advocacy page. Nobody authoritative owns it. Massive, evergreen, high-anxiety query for the exact customer this shop serves. Include Edmonton's $1,000 noise fine and the 2025 noise-camera pilot.
2. **"Where to buy E85 in Alberta / Edmonton"** — SERP is Facebook Marketplace, Kijiji, and a 2023 price-list PDF. Zero authoritative content. There is effectively no retail pump E85 in Alberta (Fury E85 via AFD Petroleum, private delivery ~$2.20/L). A definitive guide would own this instantly and pull exactly flex-fuel-tune customers.
3. **"How much does a dyno tune cost in Canada"** — SERP is entirely US forum threads (TCCoA, LS1GTO) plus one Winnipeg shop. A CAD-priced, Edmonton-anchored cost guide wins outright.
4. **Alberta modified-vehicle legality cluster** (tint, lift height, DPF deletes, insurance declaration, salvage/rebuilt, OOP inspections) — 8–10 articles forming a "Is it legal in Alberta?" content hub. Detailers and dealers currently rank with thin pages; an enthusiast-authority hub interlinks and dominates.
5. **Edmonton motorsport starter guides** — "first time at RAD Torque Raceway street legal drags," "track days near Edmonton (Stratotech, RAD road course)," "Edmonton car meets calendar." SERPs are raw event listings (MotorsportReg, AllEvents) with NO guide content. High link-magnet and community-building value.
6. **Winter × performance intersection** — cold-start behavior of tuned cars, winter storage for modified/classic cars, RWD winter daily reality, coilovers vs springs at −30 °C, winter fuel blends and octane. Almost nothing ranking speaks to modified cars in a real winter climate. This is 2240's unfair advantage: national content CANNOT speak to −40 with credibility.
7. **Classic/restomod cost & decision guides in CAD** — "classic car restoration cost Canada," "restomod vs restoration," "carb to EFI conversion," "LS swap worth it." SERPs are US shops and forums; the shop's vintage specialty makes this its E-E-A-T home turf.
8. **"Does tuning void my warranty in Canada?"** — SERP is niche US blogs and forums; Canadian consumer-protection angle is unserved.
9. **Cost-guide series in CAD** (exhaust, engine build, paint/respray, brakes, clutch) — every cost SERP checked was US-priced or forum anecdotes. "What it costs in Edmonton (2026)" series with real ranges converts and ranks.
10. **Antique plates / collector insurance / classic registration Alberta** — broker sites rank with generic pages; a practical Edmonton guide from a restoration shop with real customer examples wins and feeds the restoration funnel.

## Appendix — verified local facts for writers (use in content, cite primary sources)

- **Edmonton noise enforcement:** $1,000 first-offence fine for excessive vehicle noise (bylaw amended 2023); $50,000 automated noise-camera/microphone pilot approved by council Aug 2025; provincial approval needed for photo-radar-style noise ticketing. Vehicular noise reports via edmontonpolice.ca/trafficconcerns.
- **Alberta exhaust law (Vehicle Equipment Regulation):** muffler mandatory; widened exhaust outlets prohibited; devices increasing noise or producing flames prohibited; "loud and unnecessary noise" prohibited; 10 p.m.–7 a.m. residential disturbance provision.
- **Tint:** zero aftermarket tint on windshield (except top 10 cm strip) and front side windows; rear = unrestricted with both side mirrors; ~$155/window fines.
- **Lift:** structural components max ~500 mm (≈20 in) above ground unloaded; bumper-height rules apply.
- **Diesel deletes:** not a provincial regulatory offence in Alberta (unlike BC/ON); commercial CVIP requires OEM-equivalent emissions; federal tampering exposure remains; Park Muffler (Sherwood Park) currently ranks for this topic locally.
- **OOP inspection:** $200–$350 typical Edmonton (some advertise ~$130); 10-day repair window, free re-inspection; common fails include wipers and airbag codes; New West Partnership exemption EXCLUDES lifted/lowered vehicles.
- **Salvage→rebuilt:** licensed facility inspection; Rebuilt Vehicle Work Plan; 4+ pre-repair photos; OEM/I-CAR repair standards; certificate valid 14 days.
- **Antique plates:** vehicle 25+ years; event/parade/club use only; reduced insurance.
- **E85:** effectively no retail pump E85 in Alberta; Fury E85 race ethanol distributed by AFD Petroleum (2003 76 Ave NW, Edmonton); private delivered E85 ~$2.20/L (Fort Saskatchewan supplier); Petro-Canada Ultra 94 available at select Edmonton stations (may contain ethanol).
- **Motorsport venues:** RAD Torque Raceway (formerly Castrol Raceway) — NHRA-sanctioned 1/4-mile + road course; JB's Power Centre Street Legal Drags roughly May–late Sept; vehicles must be registered, insured, pass basic tech. Stratotech Park (Sturgeon County, ~15 min N of Edmonton) — Speed Freaks open lapping (15-min rotating groups), EMRA motorcycle events. Rocky Mountain Motorsports (Carstairs) for southern AB.
- **Events (2026 observed):** Tunerbash Edmonton season opener May 18 @ RAD; Driven Aftermarket Car Show June 6 @ West Edmonton Mall; Edmonton Motor Show April 9–12 @ Expo Centre; Exotics of Alberta June 28; All British Field Meet June 13; Edmonton Street Rod Assoc. Summer Cruise (41st annual).
- **Winter facts:** 7 °C = tire-switch threshold; Edmonton uses calcium-chloride brine (CBC-covered corrosion concerns); CAA: EVs lose 14–39% range in cold; winter-blend fuel differs (RVP/butane).
- **Insurance:** Alberta = private market; all performance/appearance mods must be declared; insurers may exclude Section C or decline; undeclared mods risk claim denial.
- **Warranty/tuning:** dealers detect via non-resettable flash counters; VW/Audi TD1 permanent flag; denial must generally relate to the damaged part.
- **Local tuning competitors (for gap analysis):** Horsepower Solutions (ProHub dyno), Player 3 Performance, SSS Motorsports (JDM), Acceleration Auto & Performance, Eurotekk (Euro), Park Muffler (exhaust/diesel, HAS a blog), Revolution Motors (HAS a blog). None run a comprehensive content program — the field is open.
