# 2240 Speed Shop — Edmonton Keyword Universe

**Prepared:** August 2, 2026
**Purpose:** Complete local keyword map for the 2240speedshop.com rebuild — feeds the SEO strategy, the 50-blog content plan, and the 5 site concepts.
**Method:** Fetched the live site + Instagram to confirm actual services, then live-sampled Google SERPs for ~24 representative queries across every cluster to classify who actually ranks (directories vs. real shops vs. nothing local).
