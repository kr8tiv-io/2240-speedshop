What should I have ready when I call?

Six photos and two facts beat a twenty-minute description every time. Shoot these in daylight before you pick up the phone and the first call turns into a real conversation about scope instead of twenty questions.

The whole car, from ten feet back

Four corners, standing far enough away to see the body lines. Close-ups hide sag, gaps and a roof that has been off.

Everything that touches the ground

Floor pans with the carpet up, trunk floor with the spare out, rockers and cab corners shot low. This is where a quote is won or lost.

Engine bay and the plate

Both fenders, plus the VIN or data plate. It settles what the car left the factory as before anybody argues about it.

The paperwork and the parts

Registration or bill of sale, any out-of-province history, and photos of the boxes of parts already bought. Half of them usually fit.
